Bike Going-to-the-Sun Road

Glacier National Park, Montana · July 1, 2022

I biked the iconic Going-to-the-Sun Road in Glacier National Park. Every year, while plow crews are still clearing snow at higher elevations, the park road opens exclusively to bikers and hikers from late June to early July. My ride clocked in at 23.22 miles with 1,827 ft of elevation gain.

"A life outdoors is a life well lived."
