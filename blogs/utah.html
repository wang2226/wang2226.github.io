Ski Utah

I skied Snowbird, Alta, Deer Valley, Park City, and Powder Mountain, checking each one off my list from National Geographic's book "100 Slopes of a Lifetime." I also experienced snowcat skiing for the first time off Lightning Ridge at Powder Mountain.

Snowbird was probably my favorite of the five Utah resorts I visited. It has beautiful views, amazing facilities, and incredibly good snow. The crowds were manageable for a resort of its size.

Alta is one of the oldest ski resorts in the country. Riding the vintage double chair and exploring its great terrain felt truly special. I got lucky, too—the day ended with an awesome powder dump.

Deer Valley is a fancy resort with great service. The grooming is top-notch, but the mountain was overly crowded.

Park City is massive. It takes at least two or three days to explore the entire resort.

Powder Mountain is seriously underrated. It has great snow, a variety of terrain, and virtually no crowds.
