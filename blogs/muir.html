Hike to Camp Muir

Mount Rainier, Washington · July 2025

Mount Rainier has always stood out on the Washington skyline and called to anyone looking for an alpine adventure. At 14,410 feet, it is both intimidating and inspiring. One of its best-known hikes leads to Camp Muir, a rugged base camp at 10,188 feet. The route feels like much more than a normal hike as it climbs through snowfields, shifting clouds, and some incredible mountain scenery.

"A life outdoors is a life well lived."
