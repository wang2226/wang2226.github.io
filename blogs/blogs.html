Uphill Pursuit

I discovered my love for the outdoors after moving to Eugene, Oregon. Since then I have chased just about every mountain-related activity I can find: skiing, mountaineering, hiking, and camping. Nothing beats standing on a snow-capped summit or skiing down its slopes.

A lot of my trip ideas come from this YouTube channel. As the REI slogan goes, "A life outdoors is a life well lived," so my goal is to explore as many beautiful places as I can.

Bucket List

  • Ski Watchman Peak at Crater Lake
  • Summit Mount St. Helens
  • Summit Mount Shasta
  • Summit Mount Rainier
  • Rim to Rim to Rim traverse at the Grand Canyon
